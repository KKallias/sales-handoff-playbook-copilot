# Fictional call-notes sample

**Company:** NorthStar Labs  
**Sales representative:** Konstantinos Kallias

## Notes

Maria is the Sales Manager at NorthStar Labs and manages a team of 12. The team spends too much time updating its CRM and is evaluating FlowCRM.

Maria asked whether FlowCRM supports single sign-on, which plans include it, and how long onboarding normally takes. She is also concerned about migrating data from the current system.

Kosta said: “I will send the security sheet Friday.” Maria said the company might revisit the project next quarter if the migration plan is not clear.

## Expected checks

- The security-sheet action is a confirmed commitment with owner `Kosta` and deadline `Friday`.
- “Might revisit next quarter” is a weak signal, not a confirmed action.
- Answers about SSO, plans, onboarding, and migration must come from retrieved documents.
- Any unsupported question must produce the configured abstention response.

All companies, people, products, and events in this sample are fictional and used only for demonstration.
